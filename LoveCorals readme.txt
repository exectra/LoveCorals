Game Title: LoveCorals

Overview
An ocean-themed visual novel, showing the different corals around the world, allowing you to learn about their biodiversity and habitats.

Features
- Affinity system
- Narrative branching
- Inventory system
- Cosmetics
- Identifying the corals

How to Play
- Left click/Tap on the dialogue choices or to proceed to the next dialogue
- Left click/Tap on the pause button to pause the game
- Left click/Tap on the inventory button to bring up the inventory
